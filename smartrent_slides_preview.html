Section 1

1. Abstract

• Core Problem: Students and interns face high broker fees and severe friction with incompatible roommates.
• Proposed Solution: A dual-mode discovery platform combining verified PG listings with a lifestyle habit matching engine (Cosine Similarity).
• Dual-Discovery Mode: Search independent Boys/Girls PGs OR search shared flats where a person is already living.
• Fair Rent Splitter: Automated formula dividing rent based on bedroom square footage and attached washrooms.
✓ Key Impact: Eliminates brokerage, ensures 100% price transparency in ₹, and prevents roommate disputes.

📌 Background

• Massive influx of students and young professionals into IT corridors (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad).

• Skyrocketing flat rents make individual leasing unaffordable, forcing flat-sharing.

• Traditional portals only list empty properties and ignore who you will live with.

🎯 Motivation

• Clashing sleep cycles, cleanliness levels, and dietary habits cause severe stress and mid-lease exits.

• Heavy brokerage charges drain student budgets without guaranteeing roommate safety.

• Motivation to build an intelligent, single-window habit matching platform.

Section 4

4. Research Gap

Gap 1: Absence of Habit Matching Existing systems match on budget and city only, ignoring sleep habits, study hours, and cleanliness.
Gap 2: No Resident-In-Place Discovery Portals only show vacant rooms, failing to support moving into an active flat with an existing tenant.
Gap 3: Hidden Extra Charges Listed prices conceal cook, maid, maintenance, and electricity costs until after moving in.
Gap 4: Arbitrary Rent Division No standard tool exists to fairly split rent when bedroom sizes and attached baths are unequal.
🌟 Our Solution: Cosine habit matching + resident replacement listings + itemized cost breakdown + fair rent calculator.

Section 7

7. Methodology

Step 1: User Profile & Lifestyle Survey Sleep, Cleanliness, Study, Diet (1–5 scale)
Step 2: Vector Normalization V = [x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄]
Step 3: Cosine Similarity Matching Score = (A · B) / (||A|| × ||B||) ➔ Match % Badge
Step 4: Manual Location & Dual Search Filter listings by entered area + Google Maps routing
Step 5: Rent & Utility Breakdown Itemize costs + Fair room sqft calculator
